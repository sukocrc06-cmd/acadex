    Geliştirici Sandbox

    Practice data analysis with sample datasets, or share your programming projects with the Acadex community.

    💡 Sandbox Developer Guide

    Who is this for? Designed for MIS students and programming enthusiasts across all 4 departments to practice coding, analytics, and software design.

    • Sample Datasets: Download raw CSV structures below to run analyses in Jupyter, Python, Excel, or SQL.
    • Share Your Projects: Publish links to your GitHub repositories or live apps so peers can check them out, view your source code, and run tests.
    • Cross-Department Showcase: All shared projects are visible public-wide to inspire other students and promote learning collaboration!

    📊 Örnek Veri Setleri

    Practice your data analysis skills with these sample datasets — perfect for MIS students or anyone curious about coding and analytics.

    Sales Dataset

    Columns: order_id, date, region, product_category, quantity, unit_price, total_amount.

    Suggested Exercise:

    Try calculating total revenue by region and by month.

    Download CSV

    Student Performance Dataset

    Columns: student_id, department, gpa, study_hours_per_week, attendance_rate, final_exam_score.

    Suggested Exercise:

    Explore the correlation between study hours and exam scores.

    Download CSV

    Customer Churn Dataset

    Columns: customer_id, tenure_months, monthly_charges, contract_type, churned.

    Suggested Exercise:

    Build a simple model or chart to see which factors correlate with churn.

    Download CSV
