Release Notes

Yenilikler / What's New

Acadex platform update history and features release notes timeline.

v1.5.0 — Planner & Highlights July 10, 2026
  • Study Planner: Added personal study tracker (Çalışma Planlayıcı) featuring upcoming exams, goals, deadline tracking with a checkbox completion, and an interactive Month Grid view.
  • Department Focus Highlights: Introduced custom blurbs tailored to MIS, Business (İşletme), Trade, and Finance curricula on the landing page.
  • Sandbox Guide: Added formatted learning guides and dataset exercises to the Developer Sandbox.
  • Onboarding Relocation: Relocated the onboarding tour restart link to the main overview tab and updated all 13 tutorial steps.
v1.4.0 — Accessibility & Trust July 09, 2026
  • Trust Signals: Linked legal disclosures and privacy options, plus added contact coordinator forms in index page.
  • Accessibility Upgrades: Implemented visual skip links, landmark elements, alt attributes, and high contrast badges.
  • Form Validation: Integrated debounced duplicate registration checkers and password strength meters.
  • Command Palette: Bound Ctrl+K / Cmd+K keys to open global quick-action searches.
v1.3.0 — Achievements & PDF Export June 28, 2026
  • Achievements System: Added fire streak badges and audio celebration chimes for key milestones.
  • PDF Downloads: Implemented clean, paginated PDF study card exporter.
  • Bulk Actions: Enabled multi-file selection and queue-based AI summary generation.
v1.2.0 — Dark Theme & Languages June 15, 2026
  • Theme Toggle: Integrated smooth dark/light theme switching.
  • Multilingual Support: Added a global Turkish and English UI translation dictionary.
v1.1.0 — Smart Interactive Canvas May 30, 2026
  • Study Notebook: Built drawing canvases supporting text entries, tables, and sticky notes.
  • Knowledge Depot: Created side storage trays to save summary blocks.
v1.0.0 — AI Exam Platform May 12, 2026
  • Practice Exams: Added classic multiple-choice, classical, and mixed mock test formats with AI hint support.
  • Instant Grading: Implemented automatic question validation and score history dashboards.
v0.9.0 — Summaries & Sharing April 25, 2026
  • AI Study Cards: Enabled customizable summarization styles (Standard, Q&A, ELI5 formats).
  • Department Feed: Created public showcase streams to publish study aids for classmates.
v0.8.0 — Core Platform Launch April 02, 2026
  • Secure Vault: Integrated Supabase Auth profile databases and secure document uploads.
  • Brand Identity: Designed the original interface and responsive navigation menu controls.
